Dear DASCH team,

Thank you for DASCH. We have been using the DR7 light curves and cutouts in a pre-registered search for
unrecorded nova eruptions among 1,211 known symbiotic and cataclysmic binaries, each compared with matched
control stars.

One result stands out. **V347 Nor = Hen 2-147** (16h14m01.1s −56°59′28″ J2000; Gaia DR3 5835411094089870592) is a
D-type symbiotic binary with a Mira and a ring nebula. Its spectrum and nebula mark it as a symbiotic nova, but
no outburst has been recorded. On the Harvard plates it appears to erupt in **February 1943**:

| Plate | Date (UT) | What we see |
|---|---|---|
| bi00717 | 1943-02-09 01:37 | nothing at the position (catalogue limit 12.93) |
| bi00722 | 1943-02-10 01:36 | nothing (limit 11.69) |
| am22958 | 1943-02-11 02:18 | strong stellar image in the cutout; catalogue: non-detection |
| rb11996 | 1943-02-11 02:30 | strong stellar image in the cutout; catalogue: non-detection |
| am22977 | 1943-02-15 02:01 | strong image; catalogue: non-detection (limit 12.97) |
| rb12036 | 1943-02-26 01:32 | catalogued B = 7.99 (saturated flag) |

The star is catalogued on 64 nights in seven series (a, am, ax, b, bi, mf, rb) through 1946. More than 1,000
deep plates from 1890–1942 and 1947–1990 show nothing comparable. We have found no report of it in the
literature.

We would be grateful for help with three things.

1. **The original plates.** Could someone examine bi00717, bi00722, am22958, rb11996 and rb12036? We want to
   confirm that the star is absent on February 9 and 10 and present at the position of Hen 2-147 from
   February 11, and that it is not a plate flaw.
2. **Catalogue misses.** In 1943, 74 of 79 plates that the catalogue logs as non-detections show the star in
   their cutouts. The bright image seems to be left unmatched or assigned to a neighbour 6.5″ away. We can
   send the plate list in case this is useful for the pipeline.
3. **Contemporary records.** Is a nova or new variable at this position noted in 1943–44, for example in the
   Harvard Announcement Cards, the Bulletins, or the plate logbooks?

We will share the light curve, cutout measurements and code, and we will credit DASCH in any publication. We are
preparing a short research note and would like it to reflect your check of the plates, whatever it shows.

With thanks and best wishes,
[Leon]
